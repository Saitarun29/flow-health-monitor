      Workflow health

      Errors are loud. A flow that quietly stops running for 3 days is the one that hurts. This watches every n8n workflow for silence, failure streaks and slowdowns.

      How health is judged

      Silent
      No successful run within 2× the expected interval, and errors don't explain the gap. The trigger stopped firing or the flow was switched off.
      Failing
      3 or more consecutive errors on the most recent runs. Usually a revoked credential or a changed API.
      Slow
      Latest run took more than 3× the median of earlier successful runs. Often comes right before timeouts.
      Healthy
      Running on cadence. The interval comes from the Schedule Trigger, or is inferred from run gaps for webhook flows.

      Connect your n8n

      Read-only scan of workflows and recent executions through the n8n public API.

      Your key is used once and not saved. It is sent only to your n8n in the X-N8N-API-KEY header for this scan, never stored or logged, and cleared from this form afterwards. A dedicated key you delete later is the safest option.

      The instance must be reachable from the public internet. On a private network, import the n8n workflow instead.
